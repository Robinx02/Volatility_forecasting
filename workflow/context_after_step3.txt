Key claims: does ML beat HAR-RV out-of-sample; which economic channels drive it (SHAP for XGBoost, TFT variable-selection weights + attention). Predictive question, not causal; null result (HAR-RV wins) is publishable. Timeline 8-10 weeks.

**DESIGN**
- Target: rv = 22-day rolling std of log USD/INR returns × √252 (annualised), known at close of t. 1-step-ahead forecast. All features lagged ≥1 day (no look-ahead).
- Features (19 ML): rv_lag1, rv_5d, rv_22d, sq_return, vix_level, vix_chg, brent_ret, dxy_level, dxy_ret, india10y, india10y_chg, us10y, us10y_chg, rate_spread, callmoney, nifty_ret, em_ret + known-future: is_mpc_day, is_fomc_day, day_of_week, month, week_of_year. ret_t is for GARCH only.
- Split: train = first 1,500 rows, expanding-window walk-forward, refit every 252 days, OOS to Oct 2026 (~3,700 rows). Never touch OOS in development.
- Baselines: GARCH(1,1), EGARCH, GJR-GARCH (arch lib, on ret_t); HAR-RV (OLS on rv_lag1/rv_5d/rv_22d only).
- ML: RF (200 trees, max_features sqrt, min_leaf 5); XGB (300, depth 4, lr .05, subsample .8, colsample .8, min_child_weight 5); LSTM (2×64, dropout .2, seq 30, StandardScaler fit on train only); TFT via PyTorch Forecasting (hidden 64, 1 head, dropout .3, hidden_continuous 32, QuantileLoss [.1,.5,.9], lr .01, batch 64, grad clip .01, lookback 63, horizon 1, early stop patience 10).
- TFT inputs: static = entity_id (constant 0); known-future = is_mpc_day, is_fomc_day, day_of_week, month, week_of_year; observed-past = all 18 market features + rv lags.
- Eval: RMSE, MAE, QLIKE (primary), Diebold-Mariano (QLIKE) vs HAR-RV and GARCH, regime split (OOS RV > 75th pct = high-vol, threshold = 0.070), Mincer-Zarnowitz. Robustness: 5-day RV target, exclude COVID (2020-01..09), market-only features, 5-day-ahead horizon.
- Interpretation: SHAP bar + VIX SHAP time series + dependence plot; TFT variable-selection weights vs SHAP; TFT mean attention vs lag; regime distance dist(t) via Bhattacharyya vs RV.
- Paper: 25-30 pp. Tables: descriptives, GARCH/HAR estimates, main accuracy, regime split, MZ. Figures: RV series w/ crises, ACF, forecast comparison, SHAP, VIX SHAP, TFT var weights, TFT attention, regime dist(t).
